The regulars

The 30 guests who came back most often. Bill Gates leads by a wide margin; the rest is mostly the journalists, analysts and investors the show leaned on for its annual conference specials.

Where are they now?

What became of the 50 most frequent guests: everyone with four or more appearances, plus six of the best-known three-timers. Current roles are as of late 2026.

Guest names, organizations, episode titles and air dates come from the volunteer Computer Chronicles Archiving Project; organizations are shown as the show credited them at the time. Birth and death dates come from Wikidata and news reports and are filled in only where the person could be matched with confidence; blank means unknown, not living. About 115 episodes don't have guest lists yet, so some appearances are missing. Episodes stream free on the Internet Archive.
