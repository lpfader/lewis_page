Computer Chronicles: The Guest List

Every guest known to have appeared on Computer Chronicles, the PBS series that covered personal computing from its early days to the dot-com era. Hosts, co-hosts and the show's own reporters are left out. Reruns are not counted as separate appearances.

Guest names, organizations and air dates come from the volunteer Computer Chronicles Archiving Project; organizations are shown as the show credited them at the time.
